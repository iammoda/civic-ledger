# Party logo sources & licensing

Logos are used strictly to IDENTIFY parties (nominative use), never to imply
endorsement. Files were sourced from Wikimedia Commons on 2026-08-14:

- liberal.svg — "Liberal Party of Canada red symbol.svg"
- conservative.svg — "Logo of the Conservative Party of Canada (2023–present).svg"
- ndp.svg — "Orange NDP logo English.svg"
- bloc.svg — "Bloc Québécois-logo.svg"
- green.svg — "Logo 2025 Green Party of Canada (cropped).svg"
- ppc.svg — "Logo of the People's Party of Canada.svg"

Commons hosts these as simple-geometry/text logos (below the threshold of
originality) and/or freely licensed uploads; trademarks remain the property
of their parties. If any party objects, the UI falls back automatically to
neutral color chips — delete the file and nothing breaks.
